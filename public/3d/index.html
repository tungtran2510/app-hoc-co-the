Thư Viện Atlas 3D

Lọc theo:

Controls

Computer

  • Left drag Rotate
  • Wheel Zoom
  • Right drag Pan
  • Double click Frame structure

Touch

  • One finger Rotate
  • Pinch Zoom
  • Two-finger drag Pan
  • Tap Select

Panels

  • Left panel: anatomical systems
  • Right panel: structure details
  • Bottom bar: actions on the selection
  • Toolbar: preset views
